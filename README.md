# NOOK 🎓

> **"Your campus. Your people. Your space."**

NOOK is a modern college social & community web application designed for students to discover what's happening around campus, connect with peers, find hackathon teammates, explore campus clubs, and manage their daily student routines.

* **NOOK**: The overall college platform.
* **LOUNGE**: The social space where students casually talk, post, discuss, joke, ask questions, find people, and hang out.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Design Principles

* **Aesthetic**: Modern, youthful, warm, social, clean, slightly playful, premium but approachable.
* **Color Palette**:
  - Canvas: Warm cream neutral (`#FAF7F2`)
  - Cards: Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Borders: Warm stone (`#EDE8E1` / `#DDD5C7`)
  - Accent: Energetic collegiate terracotta/coral (`#FF5738` / `nook-500`)
  - Text: Deep charcoal warm black (`#26221C`)
* **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans
* **Shapes**: `rounded-2xl` and `rounded-3xl` cards, subtle warm shadows, micro-interactions.

---

## 🧭 Navigation & Core Screens

### Main Navigation
- **Home**: Personalized student dashboard with Today's overview, upcoming events, and trending Lounge topics.
- **Lounge**: Casual campus conversation hangout with channel filtering and prominent post composer.
- **Explore**: Campus discovery for clubs, peer directory, trending topics, and search.
- **Events**: Curated campus events, hackathons, workshops, and sports tournaments.
- **Profile**: Student academic identity, stats, authored posts, and discussion replies.

### The Lounge Channels
- 🔥 **Trending**: Dynamic sorting by real-time student engagement (reactions + replies).
- ☕️ **General**: Casual banter, cafeteria debates, campus questions.
- 📚 **Study**: Midterms, formula cheat sheets, library spot tips, study groups.
- 👥 **Clubs**: Student organizations, photowalks, hackathon teammate recruitment.
- 🎮 **Gaming**: Valorant scrims, LAN parties, Smash tournaments.
- 🎸 **Music**: Acoustic sessions, campus playlist trade, rehearsal bookings.
- 🏀 **Sports**: 5v5 pickup basketball runs, court times, intramurals.
- ✨ **Random**: Campus pets (Kernel the cat), dorm stories, late-night campus moments.
